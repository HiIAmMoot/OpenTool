Bakes now run on the GPU, on NVIDIA and AMD cards, and are several times faster.

## Download

`OpenTool-v0.2.0-windows-x64.zip` holds `OpenTool.exe` and `OpenTool-cli.exe`, as in 0.1.0. Unzip it and run `OpenTool.exe`. The only thing to install is [Blender 5.2](https://www.blender.org/download/) or newer. The zip is 187.6 MB, up from 159.7 MB, because it now carries SlangPy, which runs the per-vertex pass on the GPU. `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` lists its parts.

## Faster bakes

- The per-vertex light, which took most of a bake's time, now runs on the GPU. Putput took 438.6 s in 0.1.0 and about 31 s on an RTX 4060 laptop GPU.
- On the same laptop's AMD Radeon 680M, Blood Gulch, Rat Race and Putput take about 40 to 60 s. The results match the NVIDIA ones: the correlation with tool's lightmaps is the same to four digits on all three maps, and Putput's light direction differs by 0.04 degrees.
- Blood Gulch on the RTX 4060 takes 19 s at Draft, 29 s at Normal and 67 s at High, against 24, 70 and 220 s in 0.1.0.
- With the CPU only, Rat Race takes 181 s at the default settings and 57 s at Normal.

## Device choice

- A Device row in the app shows the GPU it found and lets you pick Auto, NVIDIA, AMD or CPU only. Auto uses a GPU with Vulkan ray queries, NVIDIA before AMD, and otherwise the CPU. NVIDIA and AMD stop the bake with a message when no such GPU is found.
- In a settings file, `device` takes `auto`, `nvidia`, `amd` or `cpu`. The 0.1.0 names still load: `OPTIX`, `CUDA` and `HIP` mean a required NVIDIA or AMD, and `ONEAPI` means `auto`.
- After a bake the app shows what ran on what: pages, vertex pass and denoiser. `summary.json` records the same under `devices`, plus any `warnings`.

## Ray counts

- With a GPU the presets use more rays: Draft 256 per texel and 1024 per vertex, Normal 1024 and 4096, High 2048 and 16384.
- A stage that runs on the CPU uses 0.1.0's counts instead: Draft 64 and 256, Normal 256 and 1024, High 512 and 4096. The app notes this under the preset. Custom values are used as typed.
- New settings `cpu_samples` and `cpu_vertex_rays` set the CPU counts in a settings file. Left out, they follow `samples` and `vertex_rays`.

## Fixes and settings

- Two-sided sheets, where two faces sit exactly on top of each other, no longer shadow each other. Lighting on such surfaces changes in existing levels.
- A new `seed` setting (0 to 999) changes the bake's noise without changing the lighting.

## Known limits

- Windows only.
- Tested on an NVIDIA RTX 4060 laptop GPU and an AMD Radeon 680M. Intel GPUs are untested.
- The denoiser runs on the CPU on AMD, because Open Image Denoise's HIP device did not open on the Radeon 680M. The app shows a warning.
- The app is not signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
